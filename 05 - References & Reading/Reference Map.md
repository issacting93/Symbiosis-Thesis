## Research Questions

- **RQ1** How do distinct modalities of AI embodiment (virtual, humanoid, zoomorphic, distributed) shape cognitive mental models and linguistic metaphors users use to conceptualize AI agency?
- **RQ2** How does embodiment type affect user trust, rapport, and willingness to comply?
- **RQ3** How does embodiment modality influence trust repair, blame attribution, and re-engagement after critical system errors?
