# The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life — Goffman 1959

**Author:** Erving Goffman  
**Year:** 1959  
**Publisher:** Doubleday

---

## Summary
Introduces dramaturgical theory: social life is performance. People manage impressions using "front stage" / "back stage" distinctions, costumes, props, and scripted behavior. The body is a key instrument of self-presentation.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
When AI acquires embodiment, it also acquires a "front stage" — a performance surface. How embodied AI presents itself (form, movement, expression) will shape how humans interpret its agency. Goffman's framework explains why *form* matters even before any words are exchanged.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — embodiment type is a form of "costume" that triggers specific role-schemas and mental models
- **RQ2** — humans adjust compliance based on perceived role performance of the AI

## Key Concepts
- Impression management
- Dramaturgical metaphor (front/back stage)
- Role performance
- Social scripts
- Body as instrument of self-presentation
