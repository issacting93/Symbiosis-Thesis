# Tendling: Embodied AI Pillow for Emotion Regulation — Han 2026

**Authors:** Gaeul Han, Samika Agarwal, Alesia Barrios, Narangidevi Joshi, Sang-won Leigh  
**Year:** 2026  
**Venue:** CHI Extended Abstracts  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3772363.3799115

---

## Summary
Designs a soft, pillow-form embodied AI ("Tendling") for everyday emotion regulation. The soft physical form is deliberately non-humanoid — closer to a comfort object — and explores how this form shapes emotional bonding and interaction patterns.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — non-humanoid soft embodiment creates a distinct mental model (comfort object / creature, not assistant)
- **RQ2** — trust and rapport dynamics with a soft, cuddly form differ fundamentally from screen-based AI
- From Sang-won Leigh's lab — directly connected to [[Robotic Symbionts — Leigh 2018]]

## Key Concepts
- Soft robotics / tangible AI
- Emotion regulation via physical form
- Non-humanoid embodiment
- Cuddly / comfort object as AI form
