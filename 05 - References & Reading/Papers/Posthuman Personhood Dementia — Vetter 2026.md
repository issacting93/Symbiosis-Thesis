# Calls of Care: Posthuman Personhood with Conversational Agents — Vetter 2026

**Year:** 2026  
**Venue:** CHI

## Summary
Studies conversational agents in dementia care through a posthumanist lens. Argues that AI agents can "materialize personhood" — carrying and reflecting back a person's sense of self even as memory fails. From Frauenberger's lab ([[Entanglement HCI — Frauenberger 2020]]).

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Extreme but illuminating case: AI as holder of human identity. The embodiment question is acute here — what form should an AI take when its role is to *be* a kind of person for someone?
