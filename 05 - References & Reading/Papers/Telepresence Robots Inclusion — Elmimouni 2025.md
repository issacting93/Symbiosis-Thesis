# Telepresence Robots for Inclusion — Elmimouni & Sabanovic 2025

**Authors:** Houda Elmimouni, Selma Sabanovic  
**Year:** 2025  
**Venue:** CHI Extended Abstracts  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3706599.3720031

---

## Summary
Studies remote students' experiences of inclusion through telepresence robots. Finds "I am there but..." — the robot provides presence without full inclusion. Sabanovic's lab work on HRI in everyday contexts.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — telepresence robot creates a hybrid embodiment; users construct split mental models (robot body / remote self)
- **RQ2** — partial presence affects trust and rapport differently than full co-presence

## Key Concepts
- Telepresence robotics
- Remote embodiment
- Inclusion through robots
- Presence vs. participation
- Hybrid embodiment
