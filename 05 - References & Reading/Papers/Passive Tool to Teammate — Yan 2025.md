**Author:** Lixiang Yan  
**Year:** 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.14825

---

## Summary
Conceptual framework positioning AI as a "socio-cognitive teammate" in collaborative learning — moving beyond tool metaphors to partnership metaphors. Argues that agentic AI requires new frameworks for understanding its role in human activity.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — "teammate" is itself a mental model and metaphor; this paper makes that explicit
- **RQ2** — teammate framing increases engagement and compliance in collaborative tasks
