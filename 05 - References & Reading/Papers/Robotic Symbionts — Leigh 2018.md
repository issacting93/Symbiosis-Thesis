# Robotic Symbionts — Leigh 2018

**Authors:** Sang-won Leigh, Harshit Agrawal, Pattie Maes  
**Year:** 2018  
**Venue:** IEEE Pervasive Computing  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/MPRV.2018.022511241

---

## Summary
Introduces "Robotic Symbionts" — wearable robotic extensions that interweave human and machine actions. Robots act not as separate entities but as extensions of the human body, blurring the boundary between self and tool.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
**Directly names the symbiosis concept.** Leigh et al. argue for a design paradigm where robots augment without replacing human capability — mutual enhancement rather than substitution. This is the closest prior work to the Symbiosis project's design philosophy.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — the wearable form creates a distinct mental model: robot-as-extension vs. robot-as-agent
- **RQ2** — trust dynamics change when robot is *on* you vs. *next to* you
- **RQ3** — blame attribution may shift when the robot is integrated with the body

## Key Concepts
- Symbiont / symbiosis as design frame
- Wearable robotics
- Human-machine interweaving
- Pervasive computing + embodiment
