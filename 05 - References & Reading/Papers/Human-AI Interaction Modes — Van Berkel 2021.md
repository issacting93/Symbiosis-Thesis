# Human-AI Interaction: Intermittent, Continuous, Proactive — Van Berkel 2021

**Authors:** Niels Van Berkel, Mikael B. Skov, Jesper Kjeldskov  
**Year:** 2021  
**Venue:** ACM Interactions  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3486941

---

## Summary
Proposes three modes of human-AI interaction: (1) **intermittent** — user initiates on demand; (2) **continuous** — AI always present and monitoring; (3) **proactive** — AI initiates. Each mode has distinct implications for trust, privacy, and user experience.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — mode shapes mental models: proactive AI is perceived as more agent-like
- **RQ2** — continuous AI enables higher rapport but risks parasitic over-dependence

## Key Concepts
- Interaction modes (intermittent / continuous / proactive)
- AI initiative
- Ambient AI
