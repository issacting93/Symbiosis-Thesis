# Diary Methods — Bolger Davis Rafaeli 2003

**Authors:** Niall Bolger, Angelina Davis, Eshkol Rafaeli
**Year:** 2003
**Source:** Annual Review of Psychology, 54, 579–616

---

## Summary
Comprehensive review of diary methodology in psychological and social research. Covers the three main designs — interval-contingent (time-based prompts), signal-contingent (random prompts), and event-contingent (participant-triggered logs). Addresses key validity challenges: reactivity (does logging change the behaviour?), compliance, and retrospective bias. Argues diary methods capture the texture of daily life that surveys and retrospective interviews cannot reach.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Standard methodological reference for the diary component of the study. Guides the design of prompts and frequency to maximise ecological validity while minimising reactivity. Directly relevant to the decision to use event-contingent prompts for relational displacement episodes.
