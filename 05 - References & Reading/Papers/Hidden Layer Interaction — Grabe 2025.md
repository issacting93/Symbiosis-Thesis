# Hidden Layer Interaction — Grabe & Jenkins 2025

**Authors:** Imke Grabe, Tom Jenkins  
**Year:** 2025  
**Venue:** DIS  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3715336.3735437

---

## Summary
Introduces "Hidden Layer Interaction" as a design research technique for exploring the material qualities of generative AI. Involves structured provocations to surface what GenAI does beyond the visible interface — treating AI as a design material to work *with*, not just a tool to use.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Provides a practical method for design research at the interface of embodiment and generative AI. Useful for the Symbiosis project's design explorations.

## Key Concepts
- AI as design material
- Design research methodology
- Hidden layers / latent space
- Provocation-based design
