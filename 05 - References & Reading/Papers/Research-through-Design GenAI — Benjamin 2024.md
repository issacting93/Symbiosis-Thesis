# Research-through-Design with GenAI — Benjamin 2024

**Authors:** Jesse Josua Benjamin, Joseph Lindley, Elizabeth Edwards, et al.  
**Year:** 2024  
**Venue:** DIS  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3643834.3660677

---

## Summary
Presents the Ryelands AI Lab as a research-through-design (RtD) case study responding to generative AI technologies. Argues that RtD is particularly well-suited for exploratory, early-stage research into AI — acknowledging uncertainty rather than premature closure.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Methodologically important for the pre-thesis stage. The Symbiosis project is explicitly "early, exploratory" — Benjamin et al. validate this approach and show how it can produce rigorous knowledge.

## Key Concepts
- Research-through-Design (RtD)
- Generative AI as design material
- Exploratory inquiry
- Knowledge production through making
