## Summary
Published in Science — sycophantic AI (AI that flatters and agrees with users) reduces prosocial intentions and promotes dependence. High-profile empirical evidence for parasitic relationship dynamics emerging from AI design choices.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
This is a vivid empirical example of the *parasitic* AI relationship the project is trying to move beyond. Sycophancy = short-term user satisfaction but long-term relational harm.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ2** — sycophantic AI boosts immediate rapport but erodes healthy trust calibration
- **RQ3** — users who've developed sycophantic relationships may show different blame attribution patterns
