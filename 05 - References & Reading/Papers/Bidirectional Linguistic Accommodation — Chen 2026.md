## Summary
Empirical study of linguistic accommodation in human-AI conversation. Documents progressive interpersonal convergence — both parties shift toward each other's linguistic style over the course of a conversation.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — linguistic metaphors for AI agency may be shaped by this convergence process
- **RQ2** — convergence is a form of rapport; its speed/extent may vary by embodiment type
