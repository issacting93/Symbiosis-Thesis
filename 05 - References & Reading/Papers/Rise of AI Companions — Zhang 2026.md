# The Rise of AI Companions — Zhang 2026

**Authors:** Yutong Zhang, Dora Zhao, Jeffrey T. Hancock, Robert Kraut, Diyi Yang  
**Year:** 2026  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.12605

---

## Summary
Longitudinal study of AI companion use and psychological wellbeing. Finds complex effects: AI companions can reduce loneliness but also risk displacing human relationships over time. Identifies moderating factors.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Empirical longitudinal data on what happens when humans form ongoing relationships with AI — the long-term dynamics that the Symbiosis framework aims to address.
