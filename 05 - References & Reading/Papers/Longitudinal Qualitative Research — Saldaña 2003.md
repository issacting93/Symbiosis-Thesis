# Longitudinal Qualitative Research — Saldaña 2003

**Authors:** Johnny Saldaña
**Year:** 2003
**Source:** AltaMira Press

---

## Summary
A methodological framework for qualitative research that tracks change across time. Introduces longitudinal coding — identifying when phenomena increase, decrease, emerge, or disappear across data collection points. Addresses challenges of maintaining coherence across multiple time points, handling participant attrition, and representing change without reducing it to before-and-after snapshots. Argues that time is a variable, not just a backdrop.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
The primary methods reference for the diary component of the Social Dynamics of AI study. Provides coding frameworks designed specifically for time-series qualitative data — essential for tracking relational displacement, awareness development, and network change across the study period.

## Key Concepts
- Longitudinal coding
- Qualitative change
- Time as variable
- Tracking emergence and disappearance
- Panel design
