# Design Frictions for Mindful Interactions — Cox 2016

**Authors:** Anna L. Cox, Sandy J.J. Gould, Marta E. Cecchinato, Ioanna Iacovides, Ian Renfree  
**Year:** 2016  
**Venue:** CHI Extended Abstracts  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/2851581.2892410

---

## Summary
Proposes "microboundaries" — small design frictions that interrupt automatic, mindless technology use and create moments of reflection. Argues against seamless technology as the only design goal.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Directly supports the symbiotic design goal: frictionless AI leads to parasitic over-dependence. Strategic friction can maintain the human's active role in the relationship. Connects to [[Design Frictions — Cox 2016]] as a counterpart to Shneiderman's HCAI.

## Key Concepts
- Design friction / microboundaries
- Mindful interaction
- Against seamlessness
- Interruption as design tool
