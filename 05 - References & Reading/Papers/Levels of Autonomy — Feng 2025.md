# Levels of Autonomy for AI Agents — Feng 2025

**Authors:** K. J. Kevin Feng, David W. McDonald, Amy X. Zhang  
**Year:** 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.12469

---

## Summary
Proposes a levels-of-autonomy framework for AI agents, updated for the LLM era. Distinguishes between task execution autonomy, goal-setting autonomy, and value alignment — creating a nuanced spectrum beyond Parasuraman's 10-level scale.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — autonomy level shapes mental models of AI as agent
- **RQ2** — higher autonomy → higher perceived agency → potentially higher trust OR more resistance
