## Summary
Framework for deciding when AI should automate a task, collaborate with a human, or actively challenge the human's approach. Moves beyond binary "automate or not" to a nuanced taxonomy based on task type and stakes.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — task role (automator vs. collaborator vs. challenger) shapes users' mental model of AI agency
- **RQ2** — collaboration mode affects trust and compliance differently than automation mode

## Key Concepts
- Automate vs. collaborate vs. challenge
- Task-based AI design
- Human-AI collaboration taxonomy
