# AI Values and Alignment — Gabriel 2020

**Authors:** Iason Gabriel  
**Year:** 2020  
**Venue:** Minds and Machines, 30, 411–437  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s11023-020-09539-2

---

## Summary
Argues that AI value alignment is irreducibly normative rather than technical: the question is not which "true" moral principles AI should follow, but which principles are fair under conditions of widespread moral disagreement. Distinguishes four alignment targets — instructions, expressed preferences, ideal preferences, and autonomy-respecting principles — and argues for the fourth as the most defensible.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
Shifts alignment from an engineering specification problem toward a participatory design problem requiring stakeholder deliberation, not just annotation and RLHF pipelines. Gives designers language to challenge technical teams when alignment work treats values as a labeling task rather than as contested, culturally situated concepts.

## Key Concepts
- Value alignment
- Normativity under moral disagreement
- Participatory alignment
- Fair AI principles
- Autonomy-respecting design
