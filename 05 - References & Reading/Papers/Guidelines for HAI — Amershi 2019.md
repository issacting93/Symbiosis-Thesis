# Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction — Amershi 2019

**Authors:** Saleema Amershi, Dan Weld, Mihaela Vorvoreanu, et al. (Microsoft Research)  
**Year:** 2019  
**Venue:** CHI  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3290605.3300233

---

## Summary
Microsoft Research's 18 guidelines for designing human-AI systems, organized into four phases: initially, during interaction, when wrong, and over time. Empirically grounded from a large-scale survey of HAI design issues.

## Relevance to RQs
- All RQs — practical design principles for embodied AI systems
- **RQ3** — guidelines explicitly address the "when wrong" phase: how AI should handle errors

## Key Concepts
- HAI design guidelines
- Error handling in AI systems
- Graceful degradation
- User control / explainability
