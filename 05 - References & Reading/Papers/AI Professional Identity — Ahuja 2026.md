# AI Professional Identity — Ahuja 2026

**Authors:** Shivam Ahuja, Frida Pemer, Emma Mastio  
**Year:** 2026  
**Venue:** Information and Organization, 36(3)  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.infoandorg.2026.100529

---

## Summary
Tracks how AI implementation destabilises professional identity among knowledge workers, identifying three enacted strategies — Contesting, Compromising, and Connecting. Finds that professionals do not progressively stabilise around a revised self-understanding but oscillate between strategies; theorises this oscillation as constitutive of early AI adoption rather than transitional.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
Most current empirical evidence that jurisdictional and identity disruption from AI is an ongoing, uneven process rather than a transition being resolved. Maps directly onto what designers, UX researchers, and adjacent roles report anecdotally about AI adoption reshaping their professional self-understanding and relationships with other disciplines.

## Key Concepts
- Professional identity fragmentation
- Contesting / Compromising / Connecting strategies
- AI adoption as ongoing destabilisation
- Knowledge worker identity
