# Interpretative Phenomenological Analysis — Smith Flowers Larkin 2009

**Authors:** Jonathan A. Smith, Paul Flowers, Michael Larkin
**Year:** 2009
**Source:** Sage Publications

---

## Summary
Introduces and defines Interpretative Phenomenological Analysis (IPA) — a qualitative methodology for studying how people make sense of their personal and social world. IPA attends closely to individual experience and meaning-making, using interviews and diaries as primary data. Analysis proceeds through close reading, noting, and developing themes that capture both what the participant says and the researcher's interpretation of what that reveals.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Provides a rigorous analysis framework for the interview and diary data. IPA is particularly suited to making sense of how participants experience their AI relationships and how that experience changes — it handles both the descriptive and interpretive dimensions of the data without reducing individual accounts to aggregate categories.
