# Questioning the AI — Liao 2020

**Authors:** Q. Vera Liao, Daniel Gruen, Sarah Miller  
**Year:** 2020  
**Venue:** ACM CHI 2020  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376590

---

## Summary
Interviews with 20 UX practitioners building AI products. Developed an XAI Question Bank — a taxonomy of nine categories of questions users actually ask about AI behaviour (Why? Why not? What if? How to change the outcome? etc.). Showed that existing interpretability techniques map poorly onto user needs, because interpretability has been defined by what models can output, not what users need.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
Establishes that explainability is a user requirements problem, not a model output problem. Designers hold non-delegable expertise in specifying what kind of explanation is needed before any technical approach is selected. The XAI Question Bank is directly usable as a design research instrument.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — what questions users ask about AI reveals their mental models of AI agency
- **RQ2** — transparency and explainability are prerequisites for calibrated trust

## Key Concepts
- XAI (explainable AI)
- User requirements for explanations
- Interpretability gap
- Design-led AI transparency
- XAI Question Bank
