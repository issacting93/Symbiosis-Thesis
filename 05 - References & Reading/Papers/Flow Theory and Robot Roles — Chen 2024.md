# Flow Theory and Adaptive Robot Roles — Chen 2024

**Authors:** Huili Chen, Sharifa Alghowinem, Cynthia Breazeal, Hae Won Park  
**Year:** 2024  
**Venue:** HRI (ACM/IEEE)  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3610977.3634945

---

## Summary
Proposes a conceptual model for dynamic robot role adaptation using Flow Theory. In long-term multi-person HRI, the robot adapts its role (leader, follower, peer) to maintain optimal engagement. Tests this in a social robot setting.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ2** — robot role flexibility affects rapport and trust in long-term interaction
- **RQ1** — users' mental models of robot agency shift as robot role shifts
