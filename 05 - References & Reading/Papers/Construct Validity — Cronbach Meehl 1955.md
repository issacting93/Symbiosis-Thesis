# Construct Validity — Cronbach Meehl 1955

**Authors:** Lee J. Cronbach, Paul E. Meehl
**Year:** 1955
**Source:** Psychological Bulletin, 52(4), 281–302

---

## Summary
Foundational paper introducing construct validity as a framework for evaluating whether a test or measure captures the theoretical construct it claims to capture. Argues validity requires embedding measures in a nomological network — a web of lawful relationships between the construct and other variables. A construct is validated when its measures correlate appropriately with related constructs and diverge from unrelated ones.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Framework for validating the study's measures. Sociogram-based displacement measures and diary-based relational change measures are validated when they correlate with each other and with interview-based awareness measures, and do not correlate with unrelated constructs such as general life satisfaction. Essential citation when claiming the study's measures have construct validity.
