# Mechanical Sympathy — La Delfa 2024

**Authors:** Joseph La Delfa, Rachael Garrett, Airi Lampinen, Kristina Höök  
**Year:** 2024  
**Venue:** Designing Interactive Systems (DIS)  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3643834.3661514

---

## Summary
Articulates "mechanical sympathy" — a somaesthetic approach to human-machine relations where bodily, felt, and affective responses to machines are taken seriously as a design space. Draws on Höök's somaesthetics and Dreyfus's embodied cognition.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
Bridges the gap between embodiment theory and design practice. Where Frauenberger provides the philosophical frame and Leigh provides the engineering frame, La Delfa et al. provide a *design research* approach to embodiment — asking how the felt, bodily experience of using a machine creates a specific form of relation.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — somaesthetic experience shapes the mental models and metaphors users build for AI/robots
- **RQ2** — bodily rapport (not just cognitive) affects trust in embodied AI
- RQ3 — blame after failure may be somaesthetic (felt betrayal, not just reasoned)
