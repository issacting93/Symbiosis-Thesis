# Companion-Assistant Dynamics in Human-AI Relationships — Manoli 2025

**Authors:** Aikaterina Manoli, Janet V. T. Pauketat, Ali Ladak, et al.  
**Year:** 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.15905

---

## Summary
Characterizes how users navigate the companion vs. assistant roles of AI — the title quote "she's like a person but better" captures the idealized anthropomorphic mental model. Identifies distinct archetypes of human-AI relational configurations.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — mental models bifurcate along companion/assistant axis; embodiment form likely drives this split
- **RQ2** — companion framing increases rapport; assistant framing increases compliance but may reduce trust

## Key Concepts
- Companion vs. assistant role
- Mental model archetypes
- Idealization of AI
- Human-AI relationship typology
