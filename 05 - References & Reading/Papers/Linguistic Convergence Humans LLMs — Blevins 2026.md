# Accommodation Goes Both Ways — Blevins 2026

**Author:** Terra Blevins  
**Year:** 2026  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.29278

---

## Summary
Studies linguistic convergence (accommodation) between humans and LLMs. Finds bidirectional accommodation — not just users adapting to AI, but AI adapting to users. Raises questions about who shapes whom over extended interaction.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — linguistic metaphors may be partly *given* by AI through accommodation, not just constructed by users
- **RQ2** — linguistic convergence may be a mechanism of rapport-building

## Key Concepts
- Linguistic accommodation / convergence
- Communication Accommodation Theory applied to AI
- Bidirectional adaptation
- Language style matching
