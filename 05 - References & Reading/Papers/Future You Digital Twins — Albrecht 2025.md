# Future You: Digital Twins — Albrecht 2025

**Authors:** Constanze Albrecht, Chayapatr Archiwaranguprok, Rachel Poonsiriwong, et al. (Pattie Maes, Pat Pataranutaporn)  
**Year:** 2025  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.48550/ARXIV.2512.06106

---

## Summary
Designs and evaluates a multimodal AI-generated digital twin of the user's future self, aimed at strengthening future self-continuity. The avatar embodies a version of yourself decades hence.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — virtual embodiment as *self-projection*; the mental model is not of a separate agent but of an extended self
- **RQ2** — trust toward a future-self avatar may differ from trust toward an external assistant

## Key Concepts
- Digital twin / virtual embodiment
- Future self-continuity
- Avatar as embodied AI
- Multimodal AI interaction
