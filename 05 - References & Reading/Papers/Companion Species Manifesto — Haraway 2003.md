# Companion Species Manifesto — Haraway 2003

**Author:** Donna Jeanne Haraway  
**Year:** 2003  
**Publisher:** Prickly Paradigm Press  
**URL:** https://xenopraxis.net/readings/haraway_companion.pdf

---

## Summary
Haraway articulates a theory of coexistence with nonhuman others — initially about dogs and humans, but broadly about "significant otherness." Argues against the fantasy of pure human autonomy in favor of entangled, co-constitutive relationships across species and kinds.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
Haraway's "companion species" concept is philosophically foundational to the Symbiosis project's anti-parasitism, anti-transhumanist position. The manifesto argues that we are *already* shaped by our nonhuman companions — technology, animals, tools. The question is not whether we relate to nonhumans, but *how we do so well*.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — provides the philosophical vocabulary for why humans construct particular mental models and metaphors around AI "others"
- All RQs — grounds the framework in a critical, non-reductive theory of human-nonhuman relations
