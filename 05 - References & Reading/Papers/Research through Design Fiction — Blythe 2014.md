# Research through Design Fiction — Blythe 2014

**Authors:** Mark Blythe  
**Year:** 2014  
**Venue:** ACM CHI 2014  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/2556288.2557098

---

## Summary
Proposes Research through Design Fiction (RtDF) as a methodology that uses fictional scenarios and narrative artifacts as a form of empirical inquiry. Argues that fiction can generate knowledge about social and experiential dimensions of technology that conventional HCI methods miss — and that imaginary abstracts are a useful instrument for surfacing tacit assumptions.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
Provides theoretical grounding for the "Black Mirror effect" — the idea that speculative fiction functions as design research avant la lettre. Also directly relevant to the project's use of design fiction and speculative prototyping as methods for exploring embodied AI futures.

## Key Concepts
