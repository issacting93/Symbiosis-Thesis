# Interaction Ritual Chains — Collins 2004

**Authors:** Randall Collins
**Year:** 2004
**Source:** Princeton University Press

---

## Summary
Argues that face-to-face interaction generates emotional energy through rituals of co-presence, shared focus, and bodily synchrony. This emotional energy — a felt sense of solidarity, belonging, and motivation — accumulates through repeated successful interaction rituals and motivates continued participation in social groups. When rituals are degraded or substituted, emotional energy is depleted and social bonds weaken.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Provides theoretical grounding for what is lost when AI mediates or substitutes for human interaction. The loss is not merely informational but involves the emotional energy that makes social relationships meaningful and motivating. Interaction rituals with AI cannot generate the same solidarity as those with humans, even when the informational content is equivalent.
