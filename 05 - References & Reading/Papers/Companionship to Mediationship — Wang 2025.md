# Companionship to Mediationship — Wang & Leigh 2025

**Authors:** Lusi Ruth Wang, Sang-won Leigh  
**Year:** 2025  
**Venue:** DIS Companion  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3715668.3736350

---

## Summary
Rethinks the role of AI in informal older adult caregiving — from companion (focused on the AI-human dyad) to *mediator* (facilitating human-human connections through AI). Argues that AI's most valuable role may be bridging human relationships, not replacing them.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
The "mediationship" concept is a concrete manifestation of symbiosis: AI as bridge between humans, not as substitute. From Sang-won Leigh's lab (also [[Robotic Symbionts — Leigh 2018]] and [[Tendling Embodied AI — Han 2026]]).

## Key Concepts
- Mediationship (AI as mediator of human relations)
- Companionship vs. mediation
- AI in caregiving
- Human-AI-Human interaction triangle
