# Impact of GenAI on Critical Thinking — Lee 2025

**Authors:** Hao-Ping (Hank) Lee, Advait Sarkar, Lev Tankelevitch, et al. (Microsoft Research)  
**Year:** 2025  
**Venue:** CHI  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713778

---

## Summary
Survey of knowledge workers finds that GenAI use self-reportedly reduces cognitive effort and critical thinking. High AI confidence correlates with reduced user scrutiny. Raises the deskilling concern empirically.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Direct evidence for the parasitic dynamic: AI takes over cognitive work, users atrophy. Supports the project's argument for designing for symbiosis, not substitution.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — if AI reduces thinking, users may form shallower mental models of AI agency
- **RQ2** — reduced critical thinking may manifest as higher compliance
