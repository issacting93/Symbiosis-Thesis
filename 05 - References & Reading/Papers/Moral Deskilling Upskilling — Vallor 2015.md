# Moral Deskilling and Upskilling — Vallor 2015

**Author:** Shannon Vallor  
**Year:** 2015  
**Venue:** Philosophy & Technology  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s13347-014-0156-9

---

## Summary
Philosophical analysis of how technologies both erode (deskill) and cultivate (upskill) moral capacities. Argues that the ethical question is not whether technology changes us, but *how we design technology* to shape character.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Vallor's framework is the philosophical grounding for the deskilling concern. Symbiotic design should actively upskill moral and cognitive capacities, not just avoid deskilling them.

## Key Concepts
- Moral deskilling / upskilling
- Character and virtue
- Technology and ethics of care
- Designing for human flourishing
