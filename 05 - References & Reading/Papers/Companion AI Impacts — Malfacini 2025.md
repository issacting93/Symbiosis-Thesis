# Companion AI Impacts on Human Relationships — Malfacini 2025

**Author:** Kim Malfacini  
**Year:** 2025  
**Venue:** AI & Society  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-025-02318-6

## Summary
Reviews the risks and benefits of companion AI on human social relationships. Finds that while companion AI can reduce loneliness and provide support, over-attachment risks social displacement and dependency.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Maps the risk landscape for the social AI modalities. The "beneficial companion" vs. "social replacement" distinction mirrors the symbiosis vs. parasitism framing.

## Key Concepts
- Companion AI
- Social displacement
- Loneliness and AI
- Dependency risks
- Design considerations for healthy relationships
