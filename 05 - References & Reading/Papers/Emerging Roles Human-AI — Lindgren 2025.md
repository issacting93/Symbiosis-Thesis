# Emerging Roles and Relationships Among Humans and Interactive AI Systems — Lindgren 2025

**Author:** Helena Lindgren  
**Year:** 2025  
**Venue:** International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1080/10447318.2024.2435693

---

## Summary
Examines how new role configurations emerge in human-AI interaction — moving beyond simple user/tool relationships toward more complex relational arrangements (advisor, collaborator, peer). Documents role ambiguity and negotiation in practice.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — role expectations shape mental models of AI agency
- **RQ2** — unclear roles reduce rapport; clear roles increase willingness to comply

## Key Concepts
- Role emergence in HAI
- Role ambiguity
- Human-AI collaboration roles
- Relational configurations
