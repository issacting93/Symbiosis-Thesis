# Bonding with a Couchsurfing Robot — Mollen 2023

**Authors:** Joost Mollen, Peter Van Der Putten, Kate Darling  
**Year:** 2023  
**Venue:** ACM Transactions on Human-Robot Interaction  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3563702

---

## Summary
In-the-wild study of how sharing living space with a robot affects bonding. "Common locus" — sharing a physical space — increases human-robot bonding even when the robot is simple. Participants developed genuine attachment over time.

## Key Finding
Physical co-presence matters. A simple robot that *lives in your space* generates more bonding than a sophisticated one encountered only in labs.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ2** — cohabitation and shared locus dramatically increases rapport; relevant to distributed/home AI forms
- **RQ1** — domestic context shapes mental models differently than professional context
