# Egocentric and Sociocentric Measures — Marsden 2002

**Authors:** Peter V. Marsden
**Year:** 2002
**Source:** Social Networks, 24(4), 407–422

---

## Summary
Distinguishes ego-centric network analysis (studying a network from one person's position, based on one person's nominations) from sociocentric analysis (mapping the complete network of a bounded group). Discusses when each approach is appropriate, how centrality measures differ between them, and how to interpret ego network data correctly. Argues ego network methods are appropriate when whole-network data is unavailable or when the research question concerns how the world looks from one actor's position.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Methodological justification for the ego network approach in the Social Dynamics of AI study. Participants' ego networks — drawn from their own nominations and perceptions — are the appropriate unit of analysis for a study of how AI adoption changes an individual's relational world. Provides the methodological basis for the sociogram procedure.
