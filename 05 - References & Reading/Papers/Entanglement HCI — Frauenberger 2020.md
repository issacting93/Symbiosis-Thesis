# Entanglement HCI — Frauenberger 2020

**Author:** Christopher Frauenberger  
**Year:** 2020  
**Venue:** ACM Transactions on Computer-Human Interaction  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3364998

---

## Summary
Proposes "Entanglement HCI" as the next wave of human-computer interaction — moving beyond human-centered and value-centered approaches toward a posthumanist framing that acknowledges the mutual constitution of humans and technologies. Draws on Haraway, Barad, and feminist STS.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
Frauenberger provides the HCI-specific theoretical framework for the Symbiosis design philosophy. Where HCAI (Shneiderman) keeps the human at the center, Entanglement HCI asks us to think about the *relationship* as the unit of analysis — which is exactly what symbiosis requires.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — entanglement means mental models are not just about AI but about the *human-AI system* together
- All RQs — reframes what "trust" and "mental model" mean when the boundary between user and AI is blurry
