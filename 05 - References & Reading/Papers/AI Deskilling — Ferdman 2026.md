# AI Deskilling Is a Structural Problem — Ferdman 2026

**Author:** Avigail Ferdman  
**Year:** 2026  
**Venue:** AI & Society  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-025-02686-z

---

## Summary
Argues that AI deskilling (loss of human skills through AI use) is not an individual failure but a structural consequence of current AI design paradigms. Calls for redesigning AI systems to foster skill maintenance.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Central to the project's concern about parasitism. Ferdman gives the theoretical case for why symbiosis — not substitution — is necessary for human flourishing.

## Key Concepts
- Deskilling / skill atrophy
- Structural causes vs. individual blame
- Design implications for skill maintenance
