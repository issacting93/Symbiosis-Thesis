# Human-Centered AI — Shneiderman 2020

**Author:** Ben Shneiderman  
**Year:** 2020/2022  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2002.04087

---

## Summary
Proposes HCAI framework: AI systems should be reliable, safe, and trustworthy — with humans always in control. Argues against full automation and for "human-centered" design that keeps people in the loop.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ2/RQ3** — trust and control frameworks for AI systems
- Counterpoint: [[Entanglement HCI — Frauenberger 2020]] challenges the anthropocentrism of this view

## Key Concepts
- Human-centered AI
- Reliable, safe, trustworthy
- Human control / oversight
- Governance of AI systems
