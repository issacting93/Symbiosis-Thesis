# Society-in-the-Loop — Rahwan 2018

**Authors:** Iyad Rahwan  
**Year:** 2018  
**Venue:** Ethics and Information Technology, 20, 5–14  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s10676-017-9430-8

---

## Summary
Proposes the Society-in-the-Loop (SITL) framework — extending "human-in-the-loop" from individual operators to collective societal values embedded in AI systems via algorithmic social contracts. Draws on Rawlsian political philosophy to argue that AI alignment is a governance problem requiring deliberative participation, not merely a technical constraint-satisfaction problem.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
Provides the theoretical bridge between AI alignment and participatory design. Establishes that the question of what values an AI system embodies is political and social — one that design research methods (co-design, value sensitive design, stakeholder analysis) are well-placed to address. The designer is not peripheral to alignment work; they are central to it.

## Key Concepts
- Algorithmic social contract
- Society-in-the-loop
- Value alignment as governance
- Participatory AI development
