# Alone Together — Turkle 2011

**Authors:** Sherry Turkle
**Year:** 2011
**Source:** Basic Books

---

## Summary
Based on fifteen years of ethnographic research with children and adults using networked technologies and social robots. Argues that digital connection substitutes for the harder, more rewarding work of genuine human connection — people prefer the controllable, always-available digital relationship to the uncertain demands of human ones. The title captures the central observation: people are constantly connected but increasingly isolated, choosing technology's simulation of connection over its human reality.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
The closest existing qualitative body of work to the Social Dynamics of AI research. Turkle makes the same core observations through ethnography that this study seeks to formalise with network analysis. The contribution of the current study is to provide the structural, network-level evidence for Turkle's qualitative observations, and a testable framework for when and how displacement occurs.
