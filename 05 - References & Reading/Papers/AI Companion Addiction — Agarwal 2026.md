## Summary
Studies associations between AI companion role types and behavioral addiction patterns. "Frictionless love" — AI that is always available, never rejects, never demands — correlates with higher addiction risk.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
The "frictionless" AI is the extreme parasitic case. This paper provides empirical evidence that seamless, always-available companion AI is harmful — supporting the case for designed friction ([[Design Frictions — Cox 2016]]).
