# Conversational Agent vs Direct Manipulation — Jönsson 2005

**Authors:** Arne Jönsson et al.  
**Year:** 2005  
**Venue:** Speech Communication, 47(1–2), 194–207  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.specom.2005.05.009

---

## Summary
Empirical comparison of a conversational agent (CA) interface against direct manipulation (DM) for the same task. CA yielded higher task completion rates; DM was preferred by proficient users for speed and greater sense of control. Natural language lowers the floor of required expertise; direct manipulation raises the ceiling of efficiency and agency.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
Provides empirical grounding for the direct manipulation vs. natural language debate — now live again with LLM-powered product design. The finding is nuanced: the "better" paradigm depends on user expertise and task type, not the interface per se. Supports structured menus of intention as a design solution over open-ended NL input.
