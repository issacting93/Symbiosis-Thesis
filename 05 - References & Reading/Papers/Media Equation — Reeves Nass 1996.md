# Media Equation — Reeves Nass 1996

**Authors:** Byron Reeves, Clifford Nass  
**Year:** 1996  
**Venue:** Cambridge University Press (book)

---

## Summary
Presents the Media Equation thesis: people treat computers, television, and new media like real people and places — applying social and physical rules even when they know the medium is artificial. Based on a series of experiments showing users apply politeness norms, social roles, and spatial rules to screens and interfaces.

## Why It Matters for Symbiosis
The foundational empirical statement of the CASA paradigm. Establishes that social responses to technology are not aberrations but the default — the baseline against which all embodied AI design operates. Design that ignores this either inadvertently triggers social scripts or deliberately engineers them.
