# Reclaiming Conversation — Turkle 2015

**Authors:** Sherry Turkle
**Year:** 2015
**Source:** Penguin Press

---

## Summary
Focuses specifically on how smartphone and digital communication erodes face-to-face conversation, empathy development, and the capacity for solitude. Argues that conversation — with its uncertainty, interruption, and full presence — is irreplaceable for human development and cannot be replaced by text or digital communication. Based on interviews across schools, families, and workplaces. Proposes that recovering the capacity for conversation requires deliberate design choices that introduce friction back into communication.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Connects the displacement argument specifically to conversation — the mode of interaction that LLMs are now directly substituting for. Turkle's claim that conversation is irreplaceable is directly challenged by AI conversational agents; the current research tests whether the social functions of conversation (learning, solidarity, empathy) are preserved or lost when AI substitutes for it.
