## Summary
CoStage is an embodied AI co-creation system for children's performative storytelling with robots. Children collaborate with physical robots to stage narratives, blending physical embodiment with generative AI.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ1** — physical robot presence shapes how children conceptualize AI as a creative collaborator (not a tool)
- **RQ2** — performative co-creation generates high rapport; robot as equal creative partner

## Key Concepts
- Embodied AI + creative collaboration
- Robot as storytelling partner
- Children's HRI
- Physical embodiment in generative AI
