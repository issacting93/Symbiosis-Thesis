# Empathic Concern and Stories in HRI — Darling 2015

**Authors:** Kate Darling, Palash Nandy, Cynthia Breazeal  
**Year:** 2015  
**Venue:** RO-MAN  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ROMAN.2015.7333675

---

## Summary
Shows that giving robots a narrative backstory ("Johnny has always wanted to be a dancer") significantly increases empathic concern in participants — including reluctance to harm the robot. Companion to [[Who's Johnny — Darling 2015]].

## Key Finding
Narrative framing increases moral consideration regardless of robot's actual capability. The *story* matters as much as the *form*.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ2** — empathic concern links to trust and rapport; zoomorphic/humanoid robots are more easily narrativized
- **RQ3** — empathy may intensify blame attribution or emotional response after failure
