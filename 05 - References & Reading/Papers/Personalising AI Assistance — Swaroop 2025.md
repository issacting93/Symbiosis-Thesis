# Personalising AI Assistance Based on Overreliance — Swaroop 2025

**Authors:** Siddharth Swaroop, Zana Buçinca, Krzysztof Z. Gajos, Finale Doshi-Velez  
**Year:** 2025  
**Venue:** IUI  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3708359.3712128

---

## Summary
Proposes personalizing AI assistance based on a user's measured overreliance rate. Users who over-trust AI get more challenging/friction-adding interventions; those who under-trust get more reassurance. Adaptive trust calibration.

## Relevance to RQs
- **RQ2** — shows that willingness to comply is a designable variable, not fixed
- **RQ3** — post-error recovery can be personalized based on pre-existing trust patterns
