# Communal and Exchange Relationships — Clark Mills 1979

**Authors:** Margaret S. Clark, Judson Mills
**Year:** 1979 (key paper; follow-up 1993)
**Source:** Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 37(1), 12–24

---

## Summary
Distinguishes two fundamental relationship types. In communal relationships, people give benefits in response to need and feel responsible for each other's welfare. In exchange relationships, people give benefits in response to prior benefits received and track contributions carefully. The distinction governs expectations of reciprocity, emotional responsiveness, and what counts as appropriate behaviour in a relationship.

## Relevance to Symbiosis
Provides a two-category coding scheme for how participants frame their AI relationships. AI designed as a service (exchange logic) may be experienced communally over time — the shift from exchange to communal framing in diary entries signals deepening relational engagement and the activation of the relational cognitive system.
