# Human-Robot Interaction

Empirical research on how humans interact with physically embodied robots — zoomorphic, humanoid, and telepresent forms. Central to **RQ2** (trust, rapport, compliance) and **RQ3** (blame attribution, trust repair after errors).

---

## Core Papers

- [[Who's Johnny — Darling 2015]] — anthropomorphic framing in HRI; naming robots changes how people treat them
- [[Empathic Concern HRI — Darling 2015]] — stories increase empathic concern; shows emotional bonding with robots
- [[Bonding with Couchsurfing Robot — Mollen 2023]] — in-the-wild HRI; shared space affects bonding
- [[Flow Theory and Robot Roles — Chen 2024]] — adaptive robot roles across multi-person HRI; long-term engagement
- [[Telepresence Robots Inclusion — Elmimouni 2025]] — remote embodiment via robot; presence and inclusion
- Telepresence Robots Industry — Hernandez 2026 *(no paper note yet)* — industrial telepresence robots; usability findings
- Research as Care — Hsu 2025 *(no paper note yet)* — HRI design with vulnerable populations; ethics of care
- Augmenting Teams with Robots — Ren 2024 *(no paper note yet)* — robots in knowledge work; team dynamics
