# Social AI & Companionship

The social and relational dimensions of AI — companions, emotional bonds, and what it means for AI to fill social roles. Peripheral but important for understanding the risks and benefits of embodied AI forms.

## Relevance to Symbiosis Research
This theme touches the **dark side** of symbiosis — when the relationship tips into dependence (parasitism). Agarwal, Zhang, and Malfacini document the addiction/dependence risks of over-anthropomorphized companion AI.

Vetter's work on posthuman personhood connects to the [[Symbiosis & Embodiment]] theme — what does it mean for AI to take on a social *role* in a caregiving context?
