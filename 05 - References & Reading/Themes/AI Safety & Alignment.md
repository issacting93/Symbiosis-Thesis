## The Design–Safety Interface

Safety research identifies *what* can go wrong. Design research determines *how* it manifests at the interface and *what users actually need* from explanations and override mechanisms.

Key failure modes with direct interface-level implications:
- **Automation bias** — users accept AI outputs without critical evaluation; significantly modulated by UI design decisions
- **Reward hacking** — model optimizes a measurable proxy, not user's actual goal; detectable through user research
- **Distributional shift** — model fails in deployment contexts not anticipated in training
