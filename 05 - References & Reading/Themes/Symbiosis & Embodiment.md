	# Symbiosis & Embodiment

**Core theme for the Symbiosis project.** Explores how physical and virtual embodiment shapes human-AI interaction — moving beyond cyborg/transhumanist frames toward mutualistic coexistence.

> "A symbiote is different from a cyborg... Symbiosis takes inspiration from nature and asks, how do we co-exist in a way that we can benefit from having technology in our lives, but not have it take over our lives." — [[Symbiotic Design Framework]]

---

## Core Papers

- [[Robotic Symbionts — Leigh 2018]] — wearable robots that interweave human and machine action; directly frames symbiosis
- [[Companion Species Manifesto — Haraway 2003]] — theory of coexistence with nonhuman others; philosophical grounding
- [[Entanglement HCI — Frauenberger 2020]] — posthuman, more-than-human design; the "next wave" beyond human-centered
- [[Mechanical Sympathy — La Delfa 2024]] — somaesthetic human-machine relations; bodily, felt dimension of embodiment
- [[Tendling Embodied AI — Han 2026]] — AI pillow for emotion regulation; soft/tangible physical AI form
- [[CoStage Embodied AI — Song 2026]] — robot co-creator for children's storytelling; performative embodied AI
- [[Future You Digital Twins — Albrecht 2025]] — virtual body/avatar as embodied AI form
