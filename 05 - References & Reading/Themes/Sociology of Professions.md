# Sociology of Professions

Theoretical grounding for understanding how AI adoption reshapes professional identity, jurisdictional claims, and craft across design and adjacent disciplines.

## Key Framework: Jurisdictional Claims (Abbott)

Applied to the current moment:
- AI is absorbing **routine cognitive tasks** — prototyping, synthesis, pattern-matching
- Adjacent disciplines (engineering, product) are making **new jurisdictional claims** on design work
- Designers need to articulate what aspects of the work remain theirs — and why they require human judgment

## Deskilling vs. Upskilling

The deskilling thesis predicts that management will centralize judgment in tools, reducing workers to fragmented execution. Counter-evidence shows some knowledge workers use AI to upskill — handling higher-complexity tasks. The empirical picture is uneven and discipline-dependent. Ahuja 2026 shows identity oscillation rather than stable resolution.
