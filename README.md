# Symbiosis

Pre-thesis research vault on human–AI symbiosis: how embodied and conversational AI reshapes trust, relationships, cognition, and professional practice — and how design can tell a *symbiotic* relationship (where AI scaffolds people toward something, then steps back) from a *parasitic* one (where AI substitutes for and quietly displaces what it stands in for).

This is an [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) vault — markdown notes linked with `[[wikilinks]]`. Open the folder in Obsidian for backlinks and the graph view; GitHub renders the files fine, but links won't be clickable.

### `01 - Core Theory/`
The central argument: AI relationships run on a spectrum from mutualistic (bee — pollinates and is fed) to parasitic (locust — consumes without returning value), with the testable criterion being whether a technology *scaffolds* (support that's meant to fall away once a skill or tie is built) or *substitutes* (stands in for what it replaces). `Symbiotic Design (raw draft).md` is the early unfiled essay this framework grew out of; `The Metrics Problem.md` and `Data vs Stories.md`/`Levels of Relation.md` are supporting arguments.

### `02 - Embodied AI/`
Primary research — interviews with practitioners (Todd Bryant, Aarati Akkapeddi, Ahmed Ansari, Kevin Chow) on nonverbal cues and embodied AI, plus grounded-theory coding notes and a research submission writeup.

### `03 - Research & Methods/`
Methodological grounding: ecological and relational-mapping methods (ego networks, sociograms), open research questions, seminar brainstorms, a weekly research journal, and raw notebook scans from brainstorming sessions.

### `05 - References & Reading/`
The bibliography. `Papers/` holds one note per paper (full citation, summary, related papers/themes). `Themes/` holds topic nodes (e.g. *Social AI & Companionship*, *Trust & Mental Models*) that group papers and link out to the writing that uses them. `Reference Map.md` is the master index. `PDFs/` holds source PDFs for papers that also have a note in `Papers/`. `ref.bib` is the raw, unfiltered export this bibliography was filtered from.

### `06 - Writing & Chapters/`
Filed, polished writing — essays, chapter drafts, and research/position proposals, each with a themed header linking back into the bibliography.

### `08 - Anthropomorphism & Social Response/`
The anthropomorphism/social-response theme and an active DIS 2027 paper pitch (`Mapping the Missing Context.md`) studying how recently relocated international students map AI and people onto the same support-network graph. `Related Work.md` tracks citation status (confirmed / unconfirmed) for that pitch.

### `09 - Design in the Age of AI/`
See its own [README](<09 - Design in the Age of AI/README.md>) — a talk and podcast episode on UX's disciplinary rigor, with fieldwork, current deck, and archived drafts.

## Conventions

- **Wikilinks** (`[[Paper — Author Year]]`, `[[Theme Name]]`) connect papers, themes, and writing throughout the vault.
- **Paper notes** live in `05 - References & Reading/Papers/`, named `Title — Author Year.md`.
- **Theme files** in `05 - References & Reading/Themes/` carry a "Writing That Uses This Theme" section linking back to any essay/proposal/pitch that draws on them.
- Large binaries (PDFs, images, decks) are tracked via **Git LFS** — see `.gitattributes`.
