# **AI impact on communication**

Wed, 23 Sept 26

#### **Interview Context**

* Interview with Professor Akpeti, artist and technologist working across coding, performance, and visual design  
* Research focus: AI, text/verbal communication, and handmade datasets

#### **AI and Changing Communication**

* LLM mediation of everyday exchanges (email, text) now common  
* Over-reliance on LLMs as a concern, especially in pedagogy  
  * K-12 teachers report students preferring to ask LLMs over raising hands in class  
  * Chills development of bravery, questioning, and verbal interaction

#### **AI Transcription Failure in the Classroom**

* Research Methods course: remote students used auto-transcription for recorded lectures  
  * Transcript quality was poor, “chocolate wrong”  
  * Concern that mistranslated terms could compound confusion for students already disadvantaged by time zones  
  * Wished there had been an opt-out option beforehand

#### **Non-Verbal AI: Possibilities and Concerns**

* Potential applications for gesture-based AI:  
  * Performance art: real-time interaction with systems  
  * Disability justice: expanded communication options  
* More concerned than excited overall  
  * Sentiment analysis and facial recognition have long histories of bias  
  * Joy Buolamwini’s research on facial recognition, gender, and race bias cited as key example  
  * Hiring software using video interview analysis flagged as a concrete misuse case  
* Context is critical: the application determines whether excitement or caution is warranted  
  * Edge cases and inaccuracy must be addressed before deployment

#### **Open vs. Closed Model Weights**

* Open weights preferable to closed, but not a full solution  
  * Transparency on weights does not resolve bias in the base model’s training data  
  * Training data provenance is rarely disclosed even with open weights  
* Take a Media project cited: used a base model with known training data, enabling informed decisions  
* Key principle: knowing what a base model was trained on allows better judgment about its influence on the end product  
* Professor may be working on a project training custom models on photo archives; open to collaboration
